# Cwlwm Systems — Company Operating Foundation

This working playbook translates the public website into a company that can consistently sell, deliver, support and document its work. It is an internal planning artifact, not a representation that the processes have all been implemented.

## Company positioning

**Working positioning:** An independent digital and business systems studio that designs, develops, integrates and supports the technology behind better business experiences.

**Short promise:** Thoughtful digital work. Built for the way business works.

**Core principles:** clarity, practicality, ownership, reliable systems, respectful ongoing partnership.

**Avoid:** claims of a large agency team; unsupported revenue outcomes; vague "AI transformation"; promises of 24/7 support; presenting employment projects as Cwlwm clients.

## Lead qualification and intake

**Discovery questions**
1. What prompted this project now?
2. What is not working today, and for whom?
3. What software, data and people are involved?
4. Which process or experience should be different at the end?
5. What is in scope, what is out of scope, and who approves changes?
6. What budget and timing are realistic?
7. What existing access, assets and dependencies are available?
8. Who owns the work after launch?

**Intake sequence:** Inquiry -> Fit conversation -> Discovery -> Requirements / scope -> Proposal / SOW -> Approval and deposit (if applicable) -> Kickoff.

Use actual CRM objects, owner assignments and automation only after the process is finalized; the website does not imply a fully automated intake exists yet.

## Project workflow

1. Discovery, stakeholder roles and acceptance criteria.
2. Current-state map, technical architecture and source-of-truth decisions.
3. Signed scope, milestone plan and explicit client dependencies.
4. Staging build with clear iteration and change requests.
5. Technical QA, accessibility/mobile tests, security/privacy check, data validation.
6. Client UAT and acceptance.
7. Production launch with rollback plan and access review.
8. Documentation and training.
9. Handoff or an agreed ongoing support arrangement.

## Marketing and growth

Primary channels: website search presence, locally relevant business profiles, approved project case studies, professional network and referrals.

Measurement:
- Qualified inquiries by service pillar
- Fit-call to proposal conversion
- Proposal acceptance
- Margin and time by project/service
- Launch quality and post-launch issue volume
- Client satisfaction and repeat work

Use real analytics data before publishing performance claims. Keep separate company website KPIs and client business KPIs.

## Launch acceptance for website rebuild

- Public site accurately represents all five disciplines
- Navigation, footer and core routes are visually consistent
- Real proof is prominent without fabrication
- Content is easy to scan and accessible on mobile
- Calendly, email, HubSpot tracking and analytics verified
- Existing SEO paths reviewed and redirected only when safe
- SEO metadata / structured data accurate
- Terms, privacy and data-handling materials reviewed
- CI lint/typecheck/build green and full visual QA signed off
- No merge to production without approval
