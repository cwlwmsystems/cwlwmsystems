## Research standard

For each organization, complete these fields before any contact:

- Official company website URL and confirmed current status
- Exact industry, service area and business model
- Decision-maker role and legitimate public business contact path
- One specific observation backed by a dated URL or direct conversation
- One possible Cwlwm service — only if observation suggests it fits
- Whether the company may have an existing agency or internal technical owner
- Outreach compliance status and opt-out suppression
- Account priority and next research date

**Do not** infer a company has a bad website, broken software, budget, purchase intent, or data problem from a chamber listing. Do not scrape or mass-email personal contact information. Do not send automated pitches to all 30. Approach the strongest qualified accounts individually, respecting applicable commercial email and contact rules.
