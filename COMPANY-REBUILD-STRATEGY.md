# Cwlwm Systems — Company Website Rebuild

## Purpose
This is not a redesign of the previous cinematic homepage. It is a fresh company presentation and a foundation for a real, independently operated studio.

**Cwlwm Systems** is a founder-led digital and systems company that designs, builds, connects, and supports:
- Websites and digital commerce
- Custom software, internal platforms, client portals
- Automation, APIs, integrations, structured business data and reporting
- CRM and operational systems, including HubSpot/RevOps capabilities
- Digital presence, discoverability, branding and growth infrastructure
- Implementation, training, maintenance and ongoing technical partnership

### Positioning
**Thoughtful digital work. Built for the way business works.**

Keep the language clear and confident. Describe outcomes and work, not indiscriminate lists of tools. Do not overstate company headcount, employee accomplishments as Cwlwm clients, ROI results, or in-development product maturity.

### Primary audiences
1. Small-to-midsize business owner requiring website, commerce or a maintained digital presence.
2. Operations leader with complex workflow, disconnected data or repetitive work.
3. Sales/marketing leader requiring CRM, automation, reporting and connected customer systems.
4. Founder or organization seeking an accountable technical partner.

### Service architecture
Five coherent disciplines:
1. Websites & ecommerce — professional business websites, ecommerce and digital journeys.
2. Software & products — bespoke applications, operational portals, mobile-friendly field workflows.
3. Connected systems — APIs, automation, databases, CRM/HubSpot, data quality and reporting.
4. Brand & digital growth — identity, search discoverability, content, creative and analytics.
5. Ongoing partnership — technical support, site care, implementation, documentation, training and phased development.

### Work proof
- Port Petals: client ecommerce platform. Client-approved public case study exists.
- Cwlwm Field Operations: independently owned, company-agnostic development product.
- Olson & Tenglund: live information and location website; separate evolving catalog/ecommerce work.
- Community/nonprofit web projects: identify prototypes versus launched products.

No client logo photos, review quotes, conversion statistics, or screenshots without authorization and verification.

### Design principles
- Elegant and minimal, not empty. Restrained forest-green/cream palette, selective oxblood accent.
- Strong typography and content hierarchy; no arbitrary font-mixing or effects.
- Only one deliberately composed lead visual rather than decorative spinning objects.
- Meaningful interactions; default performance and accessibility over motion.
- Use authentic visuals and measured project status.
- Layouts built from spacing, grid, readable copy and high-quality project proof.
- Consistent header, footer, button styles, spacing, metadata and responsiveness.
- Build and validate behind a feature branch. Do not merge until visually approved.

### Launch constraints
Retain functioning Calendly, analytics, HubSpot tracking, legal pages and existing SEO routes; verify contact links. Publish no unsupported certifications or new service-level promises. Verify pricing before putting unreviewed amounts in prominent CTAs.

### Implementation sequence
1. Business/service architecture and design system.
2. Cohesive homepage and primary site shell.
3. Service overview and project index.
4. Rebuild service details, project case studies, founder and approach pages.
5. Align secondary routes, redirects, imagery and structured data.
6. Mobile/browser QA, CI, Vercel preview, accessibility, Lighthouse and production approval.

This branch delivers the foundation, not an unreviewed production release.
